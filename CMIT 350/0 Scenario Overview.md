## Scenario Overview
I am hired as a network consultant or IT professional to design a network infrastructure for an organization. The organization could be a small-to-medium-sized business (SMB), a school, a healthcare facility, or any other entity with specific networking needs. The scenario will provide details about the organization's size, goals, challenges, and requirements.

Based on the networking requirements described on the scenario , I use "Bacon" as a fictional organization for my assignment.
The Bacon networking configuration aims to support 75MBs data transfer across its Orlando, Toronto, and Phoenix locations, each covering 250x400 ft per floor. The network will consist of 150 VLANs connected to three subnets, with a dedicated admin subnet on the 3rd floor to manage access control. VLANs will enhance internetwork communication, while firewalls will be deployed between routers and Layer 3 switches for security. Additional security measures include password encryption, port security, and proper management of unused ports to strengthen the overall network infrastructure.
